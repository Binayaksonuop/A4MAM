Understanding
Stages of Malnutrition

Malnutrition stems from multiple root causes and manifests in different critical stages. Understanding these categories is the first step towards breaking the cycle.

SAM

Severe Acute Malnutrition

Extremely low weight for height; indicates severe, life-threatening wasting.

MAM

Moderate Acute Malnutrition

Low weight for height; weakened immunity and high risk of deteriorating to SAM.

SUW

Severe Underweight

Extremely low weight for age; reflects both chronic and acute malnutrition.

Stunted

Chronic Malnutrition

Low height for age; causes irreversible physical and cognitive damage.

Root Causes

The 2 Basic Reasons of Malnutrition

Understanding the core causes is the first step towards effective and sustainable intervention.

Insufficient Food Intake

Many children simply don't get enough food to meet their daily nutritional requirements. Poverty, food insecurity, and limited access to nutrient-dense foods are major contributors.

Poverty & Food Insecurity Families can't afford enough nutritious food
Limited Access Remote areas lack nutrient-dense food options
Monotonous Diets Reliance on staples without variety leads to deficiencies

Poor Nutrient Absorption

Even when food is available, repeated infections, intestinal parasites, and poor gut health prevent the body from absorbing essential nutrients effectively.

Repeated Infections Diarrhea and fever reduce nutrient absorption
Intestinal Parasites Worms compete for nutrients in the gut
Poor Gut Health Damaged intestinal lining reduces absorption capacity
