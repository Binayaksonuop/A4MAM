Why Spirulina Works

Spirulina is one of the most nutrient-dense foods on earth — and unlike most plant-based supplements, the body absorbs nearly all of it.

The Cellulose Wall Problem Standard plant-based iron is trapped behind hard cellulose walls, resulting in only 2-5% absorption.

Spirulina has no hard cellulose wall — so the body absorbs its nutrients directly and rapidly. This gives it up to 95% bioavailability, far higher than any standard supplement or plant-based iron source.
