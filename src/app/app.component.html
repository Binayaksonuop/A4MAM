Clinical Assessment

Anthropometric Indicators

Field-level identification of malnutrition relies on precise anthropometric measurements to classify nutritional status and guide intervention.

Stunting

Low Height for Age
Chronic Malnutrition

Reflects long-term nutritional deprivation. Height is considered more reliable than weight for field assessments as it is less prone to temporary fluctuation.

Wasting

Low Weight for Height
Acute Malnutrition

Represents rapid, short-term weight loss often caused by illness or severely inadequate food intake. Requires urgent nutritional intervention.

Underweight

Low Weight for Age
Mixed Indicator

Combines effects of both stunting and wasting. Used as a composite indicator in population-level surveys like NFHS-5.

The Cycle of Malnutrition

Malnutrition is a continuous loop that passes from mother to child, creating a legacy of systemic health challenges.

Chronic health issues and low economic productivity persist through adulthood.

The cycle repeats as the next generation enters life with the same nutritional disadvantages.
